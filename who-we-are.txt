Know Grace.
Live Grace.
Share Grace.

Welcome to Grace House. We are a small body of Christians who have deliberately kept things simple. We meet wherever. We eat together before we worship. We don't pass an offering plate, we don't take attendance, we don't ask anything of guests. We hold Christ as our covering and grace as our center, and we try to live in the freedom he purchased for us rather than under the law he freed us from. If you've been hurt by church before, we want you to know that nothing here is set up to hurt you. Sit, eat, listen. Ask any question. Leave when you like. We'll be glad you came.

Fellowship
[Time: 6:00 p.m.]
[Who: Jeremiah]
Before service we share a meal. This is our communion. In the earliest church, the Lord's Supper wasn't a ritual set apart from ordinary eating, it was the ordinary eating, done in remembrance of Him. We've kept that older shape. When we eat together, Christ is at the table with us, and we are His family made visible.

Worship
[Time: 6:30 p.m.]
[Who: Julie]
When we gather, we sing and we pray together. This is our worship, and it's all response. By great mercy God has made us His sons and daughters; the Spirit He's given us cries "Abba, Father" from within. We sing because we've been loved first. Worship is the sound of His eternal family.

Message
[Time: 6:45 p.m.]
[Who: Open]
After the meal we gather in a circle to share a message. Nick and Eli each come with something prepared so there's always a place to start. It isn't a sermon, and they are not standing over anyone; just making sure the conversation has a beginning. Whatever God has laid on your heart, bring it too. We'll weigh it together, the way the church has always done.

About the wheel
The wheel in our name is older than it looks. Early Christians wrote ΙΧΘΥΣ, the Greek word for fish, as a tiny creed: Jesus Christ, God's Son, Savior. Lay its five letters on top of each other and they make an eight-spoked wheel. Believers scratched it into stone at Ephesus, and you can still see it there today.
