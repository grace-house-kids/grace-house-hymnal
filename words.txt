# Word search themes for the kids activity sheet.
#
# The first line of each block is the theme's name (it prints beside
# WORD SEARCH). The lines under it are its words, split by commas or
# one per line. A word can have a space in it, like "Red Sea": the word
# bank shows the space, the grid runs the letters together.
#
# Words need 3 to 10 letters (the grid is only about 10 squares across),
# and each theme needs at least 6 words. 10 to 14 is best: each sheet
# uses up to 10, so extras mean more variety.
#
# Put a blank line between themes. Lines starting with # are ignored.
