On Being Small
A Sermon on Being Small
[Date: August 2026]

We keep score in the wrong currency. We count square footage and
followers, and then wonder why the arithmetic never comes out to peace.

> He must increase, but I must decrease.
> John 3:30

Three things worth trying this week:

- Do one good thing nobody finds out about.
- Let somebody else be right.
- Sit with the person nobody is sitting with.

Grace and peace,
Pastor
