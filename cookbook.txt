The Best Jalapeno Poppers
The Best Jalapeno Poppers
From: Julie
Prep: 25
Cook: 20 min
Serves: However many you want
Icon: pepper

An easy one you can’t screw up

Ingredients
15 or so Jalapeños - halved and seeded
1 lb ground sausage
Block of cream cheese
1 tbsp or so of cumin
Garlic powder to your liking
Some cayenne if you like extra heat
Couple splashes of lime juice

Directions
Preheat your oven to 400F.
In a skillet brown your ground sausage until done and drain the grease.
On low heat, add your cream cheese to the skillet of sausage, along with cumin, cayenne (if using), and garlic powder and stir until well blended.
Remove from heat and stir in your lime juice.
Fill each halved jalapeño with the filling and place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
Bake for 20 min until tops are slightly browned.
Enjoy 😊

Focaccia Bread 🥖 🕺
Focaccia Bread 🥖 🕺
From: Julie
Icon: waffle

Easy No-Knead Bread

Ingredients
1-1/4 c. lukewarm warm water
1/2 rounded tsp instant dry yeast
2 tsp honey
2-1/2 c. + 2-1/2 tbsp all-purpose flour
1/2 tbsp salt
Extra-virgin olive oil
Flaky salt or herbs to finish

Directions
You’ll need 2 large bowls - one for mixing, one for it to rise in.
In a measuring cup, stir together water and honey. In a large bowl, whisk flour, yeast, and salt together and then add water mixture. Stir until incorporated, then scrape sides of bowl clean. In the other large bowl, pour 2 tbsp of olive oil. Transfer dough into this bowl, cover with plastic wrap and either refrigerate for 8-34 hrs or leave out on countertop for a couple hours until dough rises.
Spray an 8x8 glass baking dish with olive oil cooking spray and pour an additional tbsp of oil into the dish.
Keeping the dough in the bowl, use a spatula to gather up the edges of the dough farther from you and lift up and over into the center of the bowl. Give the bowl a quarter turn and repeat around the bowl 2 times. Make sure the dough deflates while you form it into a ball.
Transfer dough to baking dish and spread it to the edges of the dish. Pour leftover oil from the bowl over the top of the dough.
Cover with plastic wrap and dish towel and let rise at room temperature for about 4 hours.
Place rack in middle of the oven and preheat to 400F. Poke the dough - if it’s ready it will spring back slowly leaving a small indentation. If it springs back quick, it’s not ready.
Dimple the dough with fingers like your aggressively playing the piano 🎹
Drizzle another tbsp of olive oil on top. You can sprinkle some flaky salt or herb seasoning over the top of the dough if you wish.
Bake focaccia until it’s puffed up and golden brown, about 25-30 min. Remove bread dish and allow to cool. 😎

Notes
🧄 For garlic bread, add some garlic powder or minced garlic in the dough mixing stage.

Slow Cooker Pot Roast
Slow Cooker Pot Roast
From: Tiffany
Prep: 15 Minutes
Cook: 6-10 hours
Icon: pot

Ingredients
1 beef roast
1 bag of baby carrots
6-10 potatoes
2 packets slow cooker seasoning
Water, enough to almost cover the roast

Directions
1. Heat a large skillet, preferably non-stick, over high heat. Spray with oil and let it get really hot — once you can see the oil starting to darken, start searing.
2. Sear each edge of the roast until crisp: all the rounded sides first, then each flat side.
3. Transfer the roast to the slow cooker. Add water (enough to almost cover the roast) and one packet of seasoning. Cover and cook on low for 10 hours, or on high for 6-7 hours.
4. About 4 hours before serving, add the potatoes, carrots, and the second seasoning packet. Stir gently and continue cooking.
5. Once the roast is fork-tender, serve with rolls or sliced bread.

Notes
Low and slow (10 hrs) gives the most tender result; high for 6-7 hrs works if you're short on time.

Amish Cinnamon Bread (no starter)
Amish Cinnamon Bread (no starter)
From: Eli
Cook: 1 hr
Serves: 2 loaves
Icon: burrito

My sister-in-law’s recipe

Ingredients
For the bread:
2 cups all purpose flour
1 (5.1 ounce) large box vanilla instant pudding
2 tsp cinnamon
1½ tsp baking powder
½ tsp baking soda
½ tsp salt
3 eggs
1 cup vegetable oil
1 cup granulated sugar
1 cup buttermilk
½ tsp vanilla extract
For the topping:
¼ cup granulated sugar
1 tsp cinnamon

Directions
Preheat oven to 325°. Grease two 8½ x 4½ loaf pans; set aside.
In a large bowl, whisk together flour, dry pudding mix, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs, oil, sugar, buttermilk, and vanilla extract.
Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients; mix until combined (do not overmix). Gently fold just to combine.
In a small bowl, combine the ¼ cup sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon for the topping.
Divide half the batter between the two loaf pans. Sprinkle some of the cinnamon sugar over it, then top with the remaining batter.
Sprinkle the rest of the cinnamon sugar over the tops of both loaves.
Bake at 325° for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted in the center of a loaf comes out clean.
Cool in pan about 10 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely and enjoy!
