Nick's Sunday Chili
Nick's Sunday Chili
From: Probably not Nick
Prep: 15 min
Cook: 2 hr
Serves: 10 to 12
Icon: chili

This one could feed the whole living room.
It doubles fine in a big blue pot.

Ingredients
- 2 lbs ground beef
- 2 onions, chopped
- 3 cans kidney beans, drained
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 1 small can tomato paste
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt and pepper

Directions
1. Brown the beef with the onions in the bottom of a heavy pot. Pour off the grease.
2. Add everything else. Stir it well.
3. Bring it up to a bubble, then turn it down as low as it goes.
4. Let it sit two hours with the lid cracked. Stir when you walk by.
5. Taste it before you serve it. It almost always wants more salt.

Notes
Cornbread on the side, sour cream and cheese on the table.
Leftovers are better on Monday.

Tiffany's Skillet Cornbread
Tiffany's Skillet Cornbread
From: Not Tiffany
Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Serves: 8
Icon: bread

Ingredients
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup bacon grease or butter

Directions
1. Put the skillet in the oven and heat both to 425.
2. Whisk the dry things in one bowl and the wet things in another.
3. Stir them together. Lumps are fine, don't beat it.
4. Pull the hot skillet out, swirl the grease around it, pour the batter in. It should hiss.
5. Bake 25 minutes, until the top is gold and it pulls away at the edge.

Notes
Cast iron only. A cake pan makes cornbread, but it doesn't make this.
