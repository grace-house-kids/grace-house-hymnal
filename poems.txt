Jonah and Thyme
This
This was the end
Of me
The beginning of You
Everything that I thought was true
A lie
Now watch a man die
To self
I thought I had hands full of help
I was hell
Not even a soul to sell
Where was I standing at the edge?
Too dark to realize my feet were past the ledge
Free fall
You ended it all
Into your arms I commend this spirit
Far from my flesh but currently near it
Where will we go?
Now that Your hand holds mine?
Only but up, back and forwards through time
This is the sign, Jonah and thyme
Eyes once blind but double the fine
A pharisee unable to walk the line
Here I am, send me
Blood on my doorpost, hands without fee
Only but up, you’re bloodied for me
Breaking
On my knees and shaking
Hold my head down while I hold my hands up
I submit
I’ll do everything but quit
Chasing You
My heart is after You
Empty of everything I thought I knew
Something new
A jar of clay,
Cracked under the Ancient of Days
A healing hand reaches out amongst the rays
Today
I am yours today
Burn it all away
Your anti-temple beneath this flesh of decay

Flesh They Say
No matter what they say
I know this is the way
Spirit now beneath this clay
Flesh they say
Infinite waves of beauty and grace
Infinite me when I behold your face
My race
Gilded with gold and gilded with lace

Cover me
(Set me free)

Beneath the crossfire
Wire to wire
Drag me down and watch me expire
My funeral pyre
Nothing of prior
In throng with the choir
Carry me higher and higher
My heart is a wildfire
(In the hands of the Rectifier)

This is just child’s play
Here now in full decay
Hands outstretched beneath the waves
Take away
I fall to my knees and rest my case
Hold onto me now in perfect embrace
Erase
Leave none of me, not even a trace

Cover me
(Set me free)

After all the time I’ve spent
Now before the advent
Here ends my descent
I repent
All the pain I underwent
Cover me now in blood spent
Begin my ascent
I repent
