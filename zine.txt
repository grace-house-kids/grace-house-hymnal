You're being discussed.
Paul kept mentioning an audience most of us forget is in the room. He tells the Ephesians that God is displaying His wisdom through the church, and that the ones watching are the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places. He tells the Corinthians they've become a spectacle to the world, to angels, and to men. Whatever else that means, it means the unseen world is paying attention, and the church it's watching is made of actual people. You included. We tend to picture the enemy as loud: the obvious temptation, the dramatic attack, the kind of evil that makes the news. We rarely picture him as patient. Quiet. Content to take notes. Tomorrow we'll hear a letter read aloud, passed from voice to voice around the circle. Before you come, sit with a question. If the powers and principalities talked about you amongst themselves, what would they say? And would any of it worry them?

Know Grace. Live Grace. Share Grace.
Welcome to Grace House. We are a small body of Christians who have deliberately kept things simple. We meet wherever. We eat together before we worship. We don't pass an offering plate, we don't take attendance, we don't ask anything of guests. We hold Christ as our covering and grace as our center, and we try to live in the freedom he purchased for us rather than under the law he freed us from. If you've been hurt by church before, we want you to know that nothing here is set up to hurt you. Sit, eat, listen. Ask any question. Leave when you like. We'll be glad you came.

Clothing Donations
Two loads of clothes, shoes, and more have already gone to the donation center, and another is ready to go out. Imagine the impact: every pair of shoes is someone walking a little easier, every bag of clothes someone a little warmer as the weather turns. If you have gently used clothes or shoes you're ready to part with, drop them in the donation box in the Kid's classroom at the Grace House. We'll launder everything and get it into the hands of folks who need it. Every item helps.

Food Donations
You can also leave non-perishable food donations right next to the clothing box in the Kid's classroom. Each week we'll either stock the blessing boxes downtown or take everything straight to the food pantry. Bring what you can, whenever you can.

Friday Fellowship
Come hang out this Friday at the Grace House around 6pm. Bring a chair, some snacks, and be ready for a great time.

Where is Communion?
Before service we share a meal. This is our communion. In the earliest church, the Lord's Supper wasn't a ritual set apart from ordinary eating, it was the ordinary eating, done in remembrance of Him. We've kept that older shape. When we eat together, Christ is at the table with us, and we are His family made visible.

How does Worship work?
When we gather, we sing and we pray together. This is our worship, and it's all response. By great mercy God has made us His sons and daughters; the Spirit He's given us cries "Abba, Father" from within. We sing because we've been loved first. Worship is the sound of His eternal family.

Who speaks?
Everyone speaks. Man, woman and child. After the meal we gather in a circle to share a message. Nick and Eli each come with something prepared so there's always a place to start. It isn't a sermon, and they are not standing over anyone; just making sure the conversation has a beginning. Whatever God has laid on your heart, bring it too. We'll weigh it together, the way the church has always done.
