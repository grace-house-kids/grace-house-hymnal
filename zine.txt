The Sunday Zine

Three Trees
Three apple trees stand in a garden. Same soil, same sun, same rain. You'd expect them to turn out the same, but they don't. One day the Gardener comes walking through, and what he finds there may surprise you. When we gather, we'll hear their story told around the circle. Before you come, sit with a question. What makes an apple tree an apple tree?

Fall Festival
What a day. The hamburgers and brats came straight from the farm, and the wagon ride through the fields couldn't have been timed better. Jeremiah shared a lesson on where goodness really comes from, a good reminder that the best things we have didn't start with us. We also got to celebrate two birthdays, Summer and Mathilda. Happy birthday to you both!

Farm Day
There were plenty of hands and plenty to do at the last farm day. We took fencing off a trailer, filled sandbags, and pulled the crop sheeting off the beds. Out behind the house, we dug up and leveled the ground for the new pad so it's ready for the forms and rock to go in. Then we took the trailer down to the Voet farm and loaded it with hay bales for the fall festival. Those bales became the seats for the kids on the wagon ride. Hard work, good company, and a few sore backs to show for it.

Mind the Mess
Renovations are still underway at the Grace House, so please watch your step and pardon the dust. When the work is done, our worship space will be nearly double its size. The church will also have a large storage area, a walk-out covered patio, and a community tool shed, and the foyer will become our donation center. Every piece of it is meant for making room: room to gather, room to share, and room for whoever comes next.

Clothing Donations
Gently used clothes and shoes are always welcome. Drop them in the donation box in the Kids' classroom at the Grace House. We'll launder everything and get it into the hands of folks who need it. Every item helps.
